INV-43523
Parent: INV-42087

Integrity classification used errors.Is against immudb sentinels. A server-returned corruption error is a raw gRPC status, and immudb compares that to a plain sentinel by message text. A wrap or an upstream reword was classified as result=error, so the integrity latch never tripped and /ready stayed healthy.

Claims:
- The auditor treats codes.Unknown and codes.Internal as an integrity failure when any ": "-delimited segment of the status message equals the pinned text of store.ErrCorruptedData or store.ErrCorruptedTxData.
- Client-local sentinels still match by identity. Transport, session, and not-found errors still do not latch.
- TestServerIntegritySentinelText fails if the pinned SDK rewords either sentence.
